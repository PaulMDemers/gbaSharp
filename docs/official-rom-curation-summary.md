## Notes

- USA releases are preferred when duplicates exist, then Europe, Japan, and other-region official releases.
- Multi-packs are allowed but lightly de-prioritized to avoid crowding out single-cart releases.
- Known emulator stress titles are deliberately included even when they currently fail, because this collection is for compatibility development.
